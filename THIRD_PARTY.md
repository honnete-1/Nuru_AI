Fonts were obtained from the Fontsource npm packages. All illustrations (the room, Nuru,
keepsakes, the logo mark) are original code-native SVG written for this project with AI
assistance. No stock or third-party artwork is used. Ambient sounds are synthesised in the
browser (Web Audio). No audio files are used.
